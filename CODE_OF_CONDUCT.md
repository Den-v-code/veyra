# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Veyra contributors and maintainers are committed to a respectful, inclusive,
and technically rigorous community. Participation is welcome regardless of
background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give and receive technical criticism constructively.
- Distinguish disagreement from misconduct.
- State uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, attribution, licenses, and responsible disclosure.
- Help newcomers understand project terminology and claim boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, or unwanted sexual attention;
- insults, demeaning language, or sustained disruption;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- plagiarism or deliberate misrepresentation of evidence;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's
moderation or private-contact channel. Reports will be handled as confidentially
as reasonably possible. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue a
warning, limit participation, or permanently exclude a participant when needed
to protect the community.

Anyone responsible for handling a report must disclose a conflict of interest
and step aside when appropriate. Good-faith reports and appeals will be
considered on their evidence and context.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces and when someone is publicly representing
the project. It does not require agreement on mathematical, philosophical, or
engineering questions; it requires that disagreement remain respectful and
evidence-focused.
