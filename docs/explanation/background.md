# Background: Why Process Before Object?

Veyra begins from the suspicion that many hard problems are difficult partly because inherited abstractions are too object-first.

Instead of assuming objects inside space, Veyra asks:

1. What distinction occurred?
2. What residue survived?
3. What transitions connect residues?
4. What recurrences close?
5. What decompositions remain stable under tests?

The hope is that number, geometry, and physical law can be reframed as different shadows of distinction, transition, and recurrence.
